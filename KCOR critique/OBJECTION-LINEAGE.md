# Objection lineage and hierarchy

This is the cross-round scientific work index, not the lead-author briefing. Each objection keeps its original x.y identifier and evidence folder. A direct return means a later review explicitly challenges a still-unresolved part of an earlier objection; a related link means a shared mechanism or evidence dependency, not that a previously corrected objection is reopened. Root-cause families organize the whole history; objections may have links across families. Implementation and independent closure are different statuses.
