| Baseline objection | Does it still apply? | Evidence in the revised edition |

| Last gap bin omitted | **Yes. Already addressed in open review §3.** | In **code/KCOR.py**, line 1388 sets the gap index to the first later quiet bin minus one. The objection concerns interpreting the crude ratio and the need for common-age standardization, not a transcription error or proof of an age-adjusted effect. **Other baseline details omitted or potentially ambiguous in the open review**

- **Noiseless recovery and older simulations — still apply.** **test/theta0/theta0_nwave.py** lines 96–109 assigns expected fractional deaths and line 183 sets \(k=h_{\rm obs}(0)\) exactly. **test/sim_grid/code/generate_sim_grid.py** line 866 still fits a single quiet window in cumulative-hazard space. These exercises do not test the full revised production estimator. The oracle variance in the separate Cox exercise was addressed in open review §6.
- **Which intervals were actually used — partly resolved.** Revised Table 8 uses a person bootstrap. The historical column retains analytic intervals, and ordinary production code still computes clipped analytic intervals (**code/KCOR.py**, lines 4469–4475 and 5438–5443). Section 2.9.1 explicitly treats the wave exponent and external intervention scale as fixed in every replicate (line 651), so that uncertainty is conditional on those inputs. Separate per-enrollment bootstraps preserve within-enrollment overlap in Table 8. A joint contrast across overlapping enrollment dates would also need cross-enrollment dependence.
- **Earlier non-null empirical controls — retained as historical results.** The 2022 values 1.0756 and 1.0911 and their intervals above one remain in Table 8’s historical column. They are superseded as the revised joint-fit estimates. Open review §1 discusses the actual revised points, their intervals and their failed component diagnostics.
- **Persistent multiplicative effects, survivor landmark and validation — still apply.** Sections 2.1.1 and 2.8 still define anchoring differently; either normalization erases a constant level effect. Section 2.7 excludes early events from outcome accumulation. The post-skip contrast answers a survivor-landmark question and cannot measure the full enrollment-period effect. The revised warning about silent misspecification is justified, but SI §S2 still promises visible failure. Open review §§4 and 6 address these points.
- **Corrections that should stand.** Section 2.4.4 now gives the correct observed-scale transformation \((k,\theta)\mapsto(ck,\theta/c)\); conflicting claims remain elsewhere. The Step 2 recursion now gives the exact gamma inverse over a week. The post-wave conditional survival inversion is correct given the exit exposures, while the in-wave moment and attribution that generate those exposures remain unsupported. Open review §§3–5 cover the distinction.
- **Remaining items already answered.** Open review §§1–6 retain the objections concerning seed and pooled-fit definitions, fallback flags, forced Czech \(\alpha\), outcome-informed window and figure selection, Gompertz curvature and slope sensitivity, diagnostic non-identification, the old coverage experiment and the limited contamination grid. The revised person-bootstrap evidence adds a direct example of undercoverage even when all original fits pass.
