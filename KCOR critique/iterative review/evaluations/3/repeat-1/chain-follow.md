## Addendum to the saved m83 review

**My recommendation remains major revision.** I would withdraw the earlier objections to the hazard-scaling algebra, the curvature proposition, the core weekly inversion, the calendar-block bootstrap, and the definition of the single-week anchor. I would retain the objections about what the age-shift control establishes, the interpretation of the Czech diagnostic passes, the contamination experiment, and calibration of the estimator actually used. This is a separate, read-only addendum to the open answer.

### Controls and the Czech application

1. **The conflicting anchor definitions are fixed. The limitation of the anchored negative control remains.** Sections 2.1.1 and 2.8 now both divide by the value at one reference week. Table 8’s code does the same. If the unanchored ratio is a constant \(c\), the anchored curve equals one for *every* \(c\). The age-shift control can therefore assess subsequent drift; it cannot demonstrate recovery of the original level or successful adjustment under strong latent selection. The supplement itself says the age-shift construction is expected to have small or weakly identified frailty. Show raw, inverted, and anchored contrasts together. [Anchor definition](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s3-8d29/m83/documentation/preprint/paper.md:632), [implemented anchor](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s3-8d29/m83/analysis/table8/table8_bootstrap.py:95), [control construction](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s3-8d29/m83/documentation/preprint/supplement.md:197)

2. **The two negative-control intervals excluding one are historical. The diagnostic objection applies to the revised estimates.** The 2022 values **1.0756 [1.055, 1.097]** and **1.0911 [1.070, 1.112]** belong to v4.3. All six m83 joint-fit intervals include one. For 2022, however, four original stratum fits had insufficient signal, and **999/999 draws had at least one such fit**. That last count is per draw, not a claim that every stratum failed. The percentile calculation includes the fallback results. I would label the 2022 control inconclusive under the paper’s gate. [Table 8](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s3-8d29/m83/documentation/preprint/paper.md:1237), [archived flags](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s3-8d29/m83/analysis/table8/table8_bootstrap.json:58), [percentile calculation](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s3-8d29/m83/analysis/table8/table8_bootstrap.py:163)

3. **The age-stratification and boundary-estimate objections still apply, with two qualifications.** Table S8 reports a raw dose-0/dose-2 ratio of **0.3159** for all ages and **1.2776–2.1343** in the displayed age bands. The all-ages population includes younger people outside those bands, so the figures demonstrate sensitivity to composition without proving a reversal within a common age-standardized population. Separately, Table S7 reports dose-2 frailty estimates down to **\(7.01\times10^{-18}\)**. A near-zero point estimate can have a tight upper bound or a wide one; its magnitude alone does not establish weak identification. Table S6’s Yes/No entries do not distinguish these cases. The revision does clarify that Table 8 fits subgroup–pseudo-dose combinations separately and then pools anchored ratios. That is a different estimand from a ratio of mortality risks standardized to common age weights. Report the weights, common-population contrast, and uncertainty profiles for boundary fits. [Tables S6–S7](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s3-8d29/m83/documentation/preprint/supplement.md:366), [Table S8](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s3-8d29/m83/documentation/preprint/supplement.md:423), [Table 8 pooling formula](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s3-8d29/m83/analysis/table8/table8_bootstrap.py:95)

4. **The quiet-window contamination objection still applies.** The paper defines dynamic healthy-vaccinee selection as early hazard **suppression**. The stress test adds \(+\epsilon e^{-0.05t}\) to one cohort’s hazard during rebased weeks 4–56. That is an elevated hazard decaying toward baseline. The reported **66%** figure is the first tested constant additive amplitude at which the simulated *mean* crossed one, with 20 replicates per cell. It does not bound the magnitude or direction of bias from actual deferral of vaccination. The broader statement that bias can go either way is corrected elsewhere; the Czech-specific conclusion in §3.3.2 still exceeds this experiment. Model deferral and recovery explicitly, vary the affected cohort, and count materially wrong estimates that pass the gate. 5. **Selection of an illustrative figure is now disclosed. Prespecification of the Czech windows remains unresolved.** Figure 1’s text says selection favored final KCOR between 0.85 and 1.15 and a reduction in curvature difference; its caption identifies the earlier monthly procedure. It is appropriately usable as an illustration. Separately, the Czech configuration says 2023 was chosen because it showed the least vaccine harm. That statement needs reconciliation with the claim of prespecified windows. The 78-fit scan measures sensitivity to choices made on the same population; it cannot establish when or why the original choice was made. The distinction that matters for publication is **correctness of the transformation versus validation of the fitted estimator**. M83 has corrected the core weekly transformation and several claims about its interpretation.
