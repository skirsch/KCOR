## Addendum to the open review of m83

**Major revision remains my recommendation.** Several objections in the archived review are resolved in this edition and should be retired. Three findings sharpen the remaining assessment: every original 2022 Table 8 contrast depends on flagged fits, changing the common Gompertz slope can move a reported contrast across 1, and the wave module still uses a different weekly baseline increment from the core fitter. This is a separate, read-only addendum to the saved open answer. The numbering follows the [archived review](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s3-8d29/turns/2-historical-blind.md).

1. **Identification, hazard scaling, and the shape diagnostic — corrected. Slope robustness — still open.** The revised proposition correctly gives \(h'=\gamma h-\theta h^2\) and explains how positive frailty can produce a straight cumulative hazard. The paper also correctly distinguishes scaling the observed hazard, which takes \((k,\theta)\) to \((ck,\theta/c)\), from scaling each individual’s baseline, which preserves \(\theta\). Section 2.6.2 now compares the inverted curve with the offset Gompertz curve. Some supplement labels still say “linearity,” but the operative criterion is correct.    The claim that a common slope error tends to cancel in ratios still needs evidence. Holding \(h,h'\) fixed changes the locally inferred variance by \(\delta\gamma/h\), which differs by cohort. I reran the 2022 Table 8 point estimator at annual slopes **0.065, 0.085, and 0.105**, holding the other settings fixed. The 2-versus-1 contrast was **1.0122, 1.0205, and 0.9940**, with **4, 4, and 6** of nine fits flagged. These are reviewer-chosen sensitivity settings. They show that the side of unity and the fallback pattern can depend on a common slope change. The supplement requests a slope check, but its displayed sensitivity heatmap varies window settings. Publish the slope grid, joint \(k,\theta\) profiles, and flags for each contrast. 4. **Weekly prediction, last gap week, and first wave week — all three specific errors are fixed. One discretization mismatch remains.** The core fits the exact integrated weekly hazard, reads the cumulative offset at re-entry, and the wave module starts from the total *before* its first modeled week. However, the wave reference adds \(ke^{\gamma s}\) per week, while the core uses the exact Gompertz increment \(ke^{\gamma s}\operatorname{expm1}(\gamma)/\gamma\). In a one-week check with \(k=0.002,\gamma=0.085/52,\theta=1,\alpha=2\), the exact no-wave input produced fitted wave exposure \(8.19\times10^{-7}\). The discrepancy is small at this slope and avoidable. Use the core increment in both places and test that a no-wave input yields zero excess.
