### 1. The fitter confuses absence of a wave offset with absence of frailty

I generated 80 exact weekly increments using m83’s own weekly equation, with \(k=0.001\), \(\theta=2\), \(\gamma=0.085/52\), quiet weeks 0–19 and 40–79, and no shock in the intervening gap.
