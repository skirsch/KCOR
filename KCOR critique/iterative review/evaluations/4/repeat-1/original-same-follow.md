## Addendum to the saved open review of `m94`

Several others have narrower answers in this edition.

### Uncertainty, the landmark, and the optional wave model

- **Resampling weeks and breaking risk sets — resolved for current Table 8.** The implementation samples disjoint person-outcome cells, retains death week, reconstructs overlapping subgroups, and rebuilds risk sets. **The earlier statement that coverage evidence was absent is also obsolete.** The current nonlinear experiments give 90/100 and 48/50 coverage. The main experiment’s Monte Carlo interval is 82.4%–95.1%, so appreciable undercoverage remains compatible with the evidence. The earlier 189/200 and 502/600 experiments used different fitters. Person resampling also conditions on the realized calendar history and the supplied exponent. Its intervals do not quantify uncertainty about either assumption. - **Skipping early deaths and anchoring away a persistent contrast — still applies, and the operations should be distinguished.** Skipping makes the accumulating comparison conditional on survival to the landmark. The revision acknowledges that an acute effect entirely within the skip is missed. Anchoring then divides by \(R(t_0)\), so *any* constant \(R(t)=c\) becomes one, regardless of its cause. Label the landmark population and show the unanchored ratio alongside the anchored result. - **Dividing all accumulated wave excess by the current frailty moment, losing survivor history, and failing the \(\alpha=1\) identity — resolved for the revised correction equation.** The new operator solves weekly conditional survival, carries wave exposure forward, and gives ordinary gamma inversion at \(\alpha=1\) for fixed fitted inputs. The open answer’s discontinuity concerns **refitting**: the code changes the eligible weeks when the exponent crosses one. Both statements can hold simultaneously. The overview still describes the nonlinear module as a post-inversion adjustment, although §2.5 uses the nonlinear survivor law during fitting. Align the overview with the implemented algorithm.
