### 4. Do fit, slope sensitivity, and quiet-window diagnostics establish depletion? **The identification objection remains. The Czech numerical example has changed.**

For any observed cumulative hazard \(H\), choosing \(B_\theta=(e^{\theta H}-1)/\theta\) reproduces the same survival for every proposed positive \(\theta\). The quiet-period Gompertz restriction supplies identification. Under proportional selection, the paper’s own formula gives \(\delta\theta=\delta\gamma/h\) at fixed observed hazard and derivative. Cancellation in a cohort ratio therefore needs evidence. In an exploratory m94 Table 8 replay, I varied the configured slope by **±20%** at fixed \(\alpha=1.18\). All six anchored point estimates moved by less than **0.008** from their configured values. That supports modest sensitivity for these six endpoints over this range. It leaves the general cancellation claim open.
