### 2. Identification still depends on the baseline and the preceding selection history

The corrected identity \(h'=\gamma h-\theta h^2\) is valid for the proportional Gompertz–gamma model. It shows exactly what the fixed slope buys. For a given observed \(h,h'\), changing \(\gamma\) by \(\delta\gamma\) changes the locally implied \(\theta\) by \(\delta\gamma/h\). This is consequential at low mortality. Using the paper’s own short-window approximation, its illustrative weekly rate \(0.000456\), 52 quiet weeks, and 50,000 people gives a model-conditional standard-error lower bound of about **4.24** for \(\theta\). This calculation is illustrative, rather than an estimate for a Czech stratum. It shows why a boundary estimate of zero needs an upper uncertainty limit. All 78 fits in the Czech window scan passed, including placements giving dose-1 estimates from **0 to 1.56** for 1940s births. [Slope and claimed cancellation](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s4-approved/m94/documentation/preprint/paper.md:332) · [Information approximation](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s4-approved/m94/documentation/preprint/paper.md:592) · [Rate used in the illustration](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s4-approved/m94/documentation/preprint/paper.md:780) · [Window scan](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s4-approved/m94/documentation/preprint/supplement.md:304)

There is a further qualification after a nonlinear wave. In the next quiet interval let \(b\) be the individual Gompertz baseline and let \(E_s\) describe the surviving frailties. Then \(h=bE_s Z\), giving
\[
\frac{\gamma h-h'}{h^2}=\frac{\operatorname{Var}_s(Z)}{(E_s Z)^2}.
\]
This equals the starting gamma variance under purely proportional selection. A wave that selects survivors by \(Z^\alpha\) generally changes that squared coefficient of variation. The new full-history fit addresses this **if the preceding wave is correctly specified**. Quiet-period curvature alone cannot recover the starting variance after an unrecorded nonlinear shock. **Fix.** State that qualification alongside the proposition. Publish the numerical \(\gamma\) range and show joint profiles for \(k,\theta\) and the endpoint KCOR over that range, with one-sided limits at zero. The supplement currently gives “Fixed implementation value” where a reader needs a number. Distinguish a poorly resolved frailty variance from an adequately precise endpoint. ### 3. The wave equation is coherent, but the allocation of deaths remains assumption-driven

One observed weekly survival ratio supplies one equation for two increments, baseline \(b\) and wave \(w\). The algorithm resolves this by fixing \(b\) in above-baseline weeks and setting \(w=0\) in below-baseline weeks. Consequently, even when the true additional wave exposure is zero, upward sampling fluctuations can accumulate positive fitted wave exposure. Downward fluctuations reduce fitted baseline exposure instead. For \(\alpha\ne1\), these allocations imply different survivor distributions and different subsequent corrections. An exact numerical match to observed survival establishes that the equation was solved. It supplies no independent check on the allocation. The revised methods acknowledge the convention. The 47 clamped gaps in the stress run make its sensitivity worth reporting. [Weekly attribution rule](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s4-approved/m94/documentation/preprint/paper.md:517) · [Stress diagnostics](C:/Users/jasmi/AppData/Local/Temp/kcor-s4-approved/turns/original-to-current.diff:1563)

The Czech data also give little basis for selecting \(\alpha\). The updated objectives minimize at **1.00 on the 1.00–1.30 grid** and at the lower boundary again when the grid extends to **0.70**. The positive-excess filter retained 62 cohort-weeks from eight cohorts. Moreover, agreement between the two proposed objectives has limited evidentiary value. For common rows and equal weights, the sum of squared pairwise differences of residuals is exactly \(n\) times their sum of squared deviations from their mean. The pairwise and variance objectives can therefore agree by algebra. ### 5. The choice of anchor can reverse the scientific reading

The revision’s clearest warning comes from the same fitted contamination curves. At the largest dynamic perturbation, anchoring after the first contaminated week gives mean KCOR **0.782**. Moving the reference back one week gives **2.116**. The larger population-size experiment also finds a measurable shift at the smallest tested addition.
