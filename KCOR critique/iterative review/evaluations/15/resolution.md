The distributed Czech workbook still displays superseded manufacturer ratios, and the ordinary point path accepts mutually impossible nested population totals. Neither finding changes the tested manuscript outputs.

## What remains

### Manufacturer results as distributed

All three `MFG_MP_*_decades` sheets in `data/Czech/KCOR.xlsx` have 2,169 rows, begin on 2020-03-02 and carry no manufacturer-status field. In `MFG_MP_2021_24_D2_decades`, the 1940 decade on 2022-12-26 still reads 1.222234 in `KCOR_MP`. The sheets can be opened directly, bypassing the corrected writer entirely. This is the same false published workbook value identified in round 14. The review found no current manufacturer estimate for that cell.

In `data/Czech/KCOR_CMR.xlsx`, 2022-06 dose 3 in the 1940s has 527,477 opening persons and 44,030 subsequent deaths in the manufacturer partition, against 527,414 and 44,026 in the ordinary cohort. Calling m173's reconciliation on those actual sheets raised the expected population-mismatch error. The difference alone does not establish which records caused it. For distribution, replace the affected sheets with the current unavailable status, or regenerate from a verified fixed-enrollment source and rerun the ordinary comparison. If retaining the old workbook for provenance, identify the affected tabs and obsolete values where a reader encounters them. This is a correction to a separate workbook, with no indicated change to the Table 8 or S6 contrasts.

### Nested totals can pass the ordinary point path

The source convention defines -2 as all ages and -60 as born in 1960 or later. Starting with the 20-week round-7 input fixture, relabel the 1950 component to 1960. Use 20,000 opening persons in each displayed birth year and dose. Supply an all-age series equal to twice the sum of both components (80,000 opening per dose), and a 1960+ series equal to six times the 1960 component (120,000 opening per dose). For dose 1 in that latter series, multiply deaths from week index 8 onward by four and reconstruct next week's opening risk by subtracting deaths. Each individual supplied series follows the death-only recurrence. The subset nevertheless has more people than its encompassing population.

With both displayed components and `KCOR_AGGREGATE_SCOPE=partial`, the shared validator checked each total against its own displayed components and found no invalid total. The ordinary point path marked both final anchored values OK, reporting 1.002719 all ages and 3.109197 born 1960+. Removing the two displayed components and using the default exhaustive scope gave the same two OK points. In that second case `aggregate_reconciliation` returned immediately because the disjoint base was empty, whereas `bootstrap_reporting.histories` rejected overlapping aggregates without their disjoint components. The discrepancy also appears without changing the subgroup deaths. The size of the estimates is not the criterion. The counts themselves prove the impossibility.

Compare nested totals wherever they coexist, per dose/week and for the corresponding counts. In a partial extract the implied undisplayed subgroup and complementary population must each form feasible risk/death histories after accounting for displayed constituents. With totals alone, an impossible pair should receive a consistent unavailable status at the point and interval entry points. Preserve unrelated individual estimates. This is a related validation gap beyond the original one-total example. The tested S6 partial replay supplies only the all-age total and six disjoint bands.

## Verification scope

The comparison used the persistent m169 and m173 candidate directories. Tests were run directly from m173 with scratch workbooks and no full bootstrap. The three-draw intervals above measure reproducibility of the software paths and carry no precision claim. The fresh S6 point comparison used the actual 2021-24 Czech sheet, the published 2024-16 endpoint, the six displayed birth bands and supplied all-age total. Both candidates produced an all-age endpoint of 1.1355510686. The 37 common result fields were identical over 1,192 rows, and m173 exported three additional fields. Independent interval and diagnostic comparisons used the delivered draws. A complete historical manufacturer rebuild from individual records and a full 999-draw rerun were outside this review.
