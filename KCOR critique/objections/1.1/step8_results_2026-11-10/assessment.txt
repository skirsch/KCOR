STEP 8: FRESH VALIDATION WITH FIXED DIAGNOSTIC RULES
Date: 2026-11-10 (America/Toronto)

Main finding
------------
The fresh simulations reproduce the main Step 7 finding. With harm during the
early baseline weeks, 114/200 runs (57%) pass all implemented checks. Their
median raw contrast is 1.309078, compared with the reference 1.049735: 24.7%
above the reference. The corresponding earlier median was 1.309151.

This is independent simulation replication within the same constructed model.
It is not validation against actual data, a claim of plausible real-world harm,
or a test of every manuscript diagnostic requirement.

Fixed design and independence
-----------------------------
Use the same 50,000 initial people per group, 200-week follow-up, constant added
hazard of 0.0001 for 53 weeks, and three cases:
  No effect: no added hazard.
  Clean baseline: harm in rebased weeks 5-57.
  Affected baseline: harm in rebased weeks 0-52.
The initial two weeks are skipped; baseline estimation uses rebased weeks 0-3
and the nominal quiet window is weeks 58-110.

There are 200 fresh paired repetitions, each with all three cases. Random seed
sequences use [20261109, 2, repetition, cohort]; earlier calibration and evaluation
used branches 0 and 1. Within a repetition the comparator is shared and the
affected-group cases share random streams. These are 200 paired repetitions,
not 600 independent case comparisons.

All five diagnostic cutoffs are read unchanged from Steps 6 and 7. There is no
calibration, tuning, early stopping, or outcome-dependent exclusion in this run.
The additional checks remain quiet-window boundary sensitivity, skipped-week
sensitivity, fitted mortality-growth sensitivity, and corrected-curve linearity
in Gompertz-transformed time. Acceptance requires all four plus the original
residual check and applicable fitting eligibility conditions.

The design was saved before replays and fresh outcomes. Before generating fresh
draws, the new adapter reproduced earlier repetitions 0, 99, and 199 for all
three cases: nine cases, 117 fitting variants, diagnostic scores, and decisions.
It reuses the original binomial simulator and production fitter/inversion.

No formal test of differences between the two simulation batches is claimed.

Before diagnostic selection, affected-baseline estimates exceed the paired
clean-baseline estimates in 199/200 fresh repetitions. The median within-pair
increase is 0.264373 contrast units. This corroborates an additional effect of
the timing change beyond the broad uncertainty seen in the no-effect results.

The residual check alone accepts 187/200 no-effect, 187/200 clean-baseline, and
199/200 affected-baseline runs. Additional checks provide the rejection above;
the residual check alone remains ineffective for this contamination pattern.

Interpretation and remaining scope
----------------------------------
The earlier result is reproduced under independent random draws and unchanged
rules: early-baseline contamination can yield substantial overcorrection that
survives this set of residual, stability, and shape checks. The inference stays
conditional on the tested generator, effect strength, sample size, and rules.

This does not establish a failure passing the complete manuscript procedure.
The numerical cutoffs and some perturbation magnitudes are exploratory; there
is no promised overall 5% false-alarm rate. One quiet window cannot test omission
or coherence across windows, and the optional epidemic module is inactive.
The inherited effect's real-data plausibility and applicability remain untested.

As previously recorded, shifting the quiet-window start earlier can introduce
four harm weeks in the clean-baseline case; affected-baseline harm stays outside
all shifted quiet windows. Skipped-week sensitivity changes the included
follow-up, so it is a stability check, not error against an unchanged target.

This completes the planned simulation work. One bounded step remains: assemble
the assessment of objection 1 across the original audit, expected-count timing
experiments, and random-death diagnostic experiments. No further scenarios or
cutoff tuning are planned as part of that write-up.
