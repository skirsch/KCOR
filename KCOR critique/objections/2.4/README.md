# 2.4 First week of a wave

Evaluation finding N4 from the [November 21, 2026 review](<../../iterative review/evaluations/2/README.md>).

Corrected in iteration 2 and confirmed after checking the original intent. The original plan specified the first inside-wave bin as the reference and stated continuity at wave entry as its aim. The recorded rationale gives no separate purpose for leaving that week's excess uncorrected. The author confirmed that the first modeled week is part of the wave. The revised no-wave reference meets the observed curve immediately before that week and includes its fitted baseline increment. This preserves continuity at entry and makes the first week eligible for the same positive-excess correction as later weeks.
